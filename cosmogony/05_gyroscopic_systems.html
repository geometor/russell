Chapter V: Gyroscopic Precession & The Evolution of Atomic and Stellar Systems¶

Why True Spheres Only Exist at $90^\circ$ Wave Amplitude (Lessons 42–43, Figs. 95–104)

Both atomic systems and stellar/planetary systems evolve along the same gyroscopic wave path from stillness (0) to wave amplitude (4±) and back to stillness (0):

  • Centripetal Winding (`1+ -> 2+ -> 3+`): Beginning as an expanded inert-gas ring in space (0), centripetal compression winds the ring through prolate (polar-elongated) spheroids whose axes precess toward the wave amplitude perpendicular.

  • The True Sphere at Wave Amplitude (`4+ / 0 / 4-`): Only when a system locks squarely upon the $90^\circ$ wave amplitude crest does its central nucleus become a true incandescent sphere (such as Carbon in the elements or a mature Sun in a solar system), with its planets revolving in its equatorial plane (Fig. 103).

  • Centrifugal Unwinding (`3- -> 2- -> 1- -> 0`): Past the amplitude crest, centrifugal radiation flattens the sphere at its poles and bulges its equator into oblate spheroids, ringed planets (like Saturn), and ring nebulae (like the Ring Nebula in Lyra or Hydra), unwinding back into the inert gases of space.
