Chapter IV: Spherical Light-Lenses, Octant Curvature Reversal & Gravity¶

How Curved Radii Focus Light into Centers of Gravity and Unwind into Rings (Lesson 46)

In Lesson 46 (Figs. 170–172), Russell demonstrates that every spherical wave-field acts as an optical Light-Sphere Lens divided into eight sections by the three orthogonal equators of zero curvature:

  1. Curvature Reversal across Zero Equators (`Fig. 170`): As light rays pass from one octant to the next across any of the three orthogonal zero-curvature equators, they lose their curvature at the plane of the equator and reverse it in the opposite octant.

  2. Gravity as Focused Light Compression (`Fig. 171`): All non-equatorial radii curve inward toward the poles to focus at the center of the lens, creating a center of gravity where light compression and heat reach their maximum.

  3. Coronas Become Rings and Rings Become Systems (`Fig. 172`): Maximum generation (inward winding) occurs at the poles, while maximum radiation (outward thrust) occurs along the united equator—casting off equatorial coronas that condense into nebulous rings and eventually into orbiting planets or electrons.
