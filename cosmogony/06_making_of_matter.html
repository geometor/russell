Chapter VI: The Making of Matter — The Nine-Octave Periodic Wave of the Elements¶

Inert Gas Master-Tones, Octave Locking Points, and Carbon at the Crest (Lesson 47, Figs. 175–180)

In Lesson 47 (We Now Consider the Making of Matter), Russell applies the Cube-Sphere wave-field directly to chemistry and atomic structure, contrasting the traditional Mendeleev table (Fig. 175) with his Nine-Octave Periodic Chart of the Elements (Fig. 177, copyrighted in 1926).

  1. Inert Gases as Still Magnetic Master-Tones (`0`): The inert gases (Helium, Neon, Argon, Krypton, Xenon, Radon) are not merely “unreactive elements”—they are the zero-equator recording seeds of Still Magnetic Light from which each octave of moving matter unfolds and into which it disappears.

  2. Four Pairs of Gyroscopic Locking Points (`1±, 2±, 3±, 4±`): In each octave, four centripetal (+) integrating pressure locks (1+ Alkali metals, 2+ Alkaline earths, 3+ Boron/Aluminum group) wind upward in density and melting point toward the `4±` Amplitude Crest (Carbon, Silicon, Titanium/Cobalt, Zirconium/Rhodium, Hafnium/Lutetium), while four centrifugal (-) disintegrating locks (3- Pnictogens, 2- Chalcogens, 1- Halogens) unwind back to the next Inert Gas (0).
