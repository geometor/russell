counterparts alternately.

                         THE MIRACLE OF GROWTH

Instead of thinking of it as something which is GROWING try to imagine it as a positive
image which is being photographed into its positive form from the negative form which
surrounds it.
     Now try to think of it as a "time exposure". Just as it takes time to project light
through a two dimensional negative to print a positive so, likewise, it takes time for light-
waves in space to "gravitate" toward the center of desire which the seed is.
     Consider your own body, for example. It took millions of years of desire of your
Soul to build your body, one little thought at a time,--one little desire at a time. You
cannot reproduce it instantaneously, it takes time.
