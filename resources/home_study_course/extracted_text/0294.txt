     QUESTION. "Does cremation have any disastrous effect upon reincarnating
bodies?"
     ANSWER. Not the slightest. The body which is discarded does not return. It is
replaced, just as your body has always been replaced from day to day.
     You have not one particle of the same body you were born with. You are constantly
discarding it and replacing it with similar states of motion. If you were not constantly
discarding your body it might weigh a hundred tons in a lifetime.
     Cremation of your whole body would have no more effect upon future bodies
assembled by desire in you, than the burning of an amputated arm would have.
     Reincarnation does not mean the rebirth of the same body--it means the assembling
of the same states of motion which constitute the pattern of that body.
