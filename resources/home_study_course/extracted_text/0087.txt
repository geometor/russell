     In the THINKER I knew the FATHER-MOTHERHOOD IDEA divided into
FATHERHOOD AND MOTHERHOOD as a dynamic expression of the static Mind-
imagined idea. The FATHER idea is expressed by the inward pulsation of the heartbeat
of God's thinking and the MOTHER idea by the outward pulsation.
     One pulsation starts from rest in the magnetic Light at a zero point of desire, seeks a
zero point of rest in the Light at the fulfillment of desire and returns to the zero point of
its beginning to repeat it in endless sequences.
     The important thing for you to comprehend regarding the above is that every
moment of your life you are attempting to fulfill some desire. Every effort you make
toward that fulfillment is a positive action starting from rest to seek a point of rest. Your
positive action expresses FATHERHOOD.
