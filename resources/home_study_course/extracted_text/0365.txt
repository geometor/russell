If you place some iron filings between two bar magnets you will find that they quickly
form a sphere. If you approach that sphere with a needle you will find that the needle
points toward the center of the sphere, whereas it would point toward either pole of the
magnet if the iron filings were removed. By studying Fig. 71 on page 523, and Fig. 80
together you will more readily understand why the earth is not ONE magnet but is the
uniting of two halves of two magnets.
