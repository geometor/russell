     You rejoice when God gives birth to life, yet you deeply grieve when you give
rebirth to new life--for that is what death is.
     It will be many decades before man as a mass comprehends this idea which is
commonplace thinking to cosmic man, but you, in whom the seed of cosmic thinking is
being sown, will go to your neighbor and sow it in his Consciousness, and he in turn will
do likewise, until man in the mass will be transformed from Barbaric man who still
crucifies love to cosmic man who is love.
     Tomorrow we will give thought to the deeper meaning of prayer.
