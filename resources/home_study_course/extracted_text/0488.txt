     Do you think that your body can die? If so, observe the "soul" of radium leaving its
depolarizing body in rays of light which are inert gases. You can actually see them in a
spinthariscope. Radium will have another body when its depolarization reverses--as all

violent explosion. If so, try to "kill" a filament of tungsten by passing a hundred thousand

that tungsten body which cannot
