                            Figure 5 - Concentration and Decentration

    Fig. 5 represents concentration of light rays to a point. Light rays thus concentrated
through a lens will burn a hole through paper or fabrics.
    Light rays proceeding in the opposite direction will expand and cool by decentrating
toward space.
    "The whole physical universe of forming growing bodies is due to the fast motion of
concentrative thinking. Conversely all degenerating and decaying bodies are due to the
expansion of decentrative thinking."
