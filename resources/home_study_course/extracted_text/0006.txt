     It is my hope that each reader will so clearly comprehend each part of this whole
picture of God's cosmic plan that each one can so put them together in his comprehension
that he too may vision the many parts as One Whole. He who can so strongly see the
Oneness of all the seeming many parts of God's plan may make of his own life a divine
extension of that One and thus become master Creator of his own creating universe.

              Walter Russell, Sc. D.
