The generic term which we will henceforth use for the pairs of opposite expressions of
any idea is POLARITY, or NORTH and SOUTH.

The two terms used to distinguish one extreme of polarity from the other extreme are
POSITIVE and NEGATIVE.

CONCENTRATION is positive. DECENTRATION is negative. The MALE is positive.
The FEMALE is negative.
