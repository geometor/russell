     ACTION AND REACTION ARE EQUAL AND OPPOSITE, AND ARE

REVERSE, THE REACTION BECOMING THE ACTION AND THE ACTION THE
REACTION.

Trinity.

     The more you understand The Divine Trinity the more you will realize that Creation

forms.

that each of the seeming divided pairs of opposites in the universe are born from the

     This may seem amazing but it is true. Life and death are born from each other and
become the other, just as heat and cold, or compression and expansion likewise do.

seem to add to it by subtracting from it that which you add. Likewise if you think you are
