               THIS IS A TWO-WAY RADIAL UNIVERSE

Because of the two opposed directions of concentrative-decentrative thinking the entire
visible universe is composed of spheres. No other form is possible because of it. We shall
later see that the cube of space is the only possible form in the invisible universe, and for
the same reasons.
     We have not yet reached the point where this can be made clear, for we have not
arrived at the point where we can begin to discuss waves, wave fields and the workings
of opposed pressures in creating a curved universe of two-way spiral interweavings.
When we do reach that point God's simple plan will seem more simple to you because of
the impossibility of exceeding the principle of The Divine Trinity in any effect
whatsoever.
