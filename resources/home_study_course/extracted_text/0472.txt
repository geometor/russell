     WHAT ARE THESE SO-CALLED MAGNETIC LINES OF
                       FORCE?

It now becomes necessary to explain away the so-called magnetic lines of force which do
exist in all electric phenomena but are not what they are supposed to be, as their name
implies.
     These curved lines in the sun, or earth, or between the poles of a bar magnet have no
relation whatsoever to a so-called non-existent "magnetic force". Magnetic poles are
balancers and measurers of polarized electric effects. They belong to the zero of
equilibrium from which equators of zero curvature extend to bound, control and balance
all gravitational and radiational curvatures.
     What, then, are these so-called curved magnetic lines of force? A familiar
demonstration is given to students by placing some iron filings on a piece of paper over a
magnet, then agitating the paper until the iron filings curve OUTWARD from its
centering radius as you see them connecting north and south poles in Figures 168 and
161.
