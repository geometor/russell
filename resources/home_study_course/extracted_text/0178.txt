Meditate deeply upon this subject and you will find that there is no part of this journey of
the tree from its appearance to its disappearance, that you could call life without as
appropriately and truthfully calling it death.
     Nor is there a part of it that you could call its beginning or its end.
     Nor is the fulcrum which the tree used to extend itself into a tree any different from
the fulcrum you use to extend yourself into a man. Nor are you freed from your roots
underground because you can walk away from them, for you are as much bound to them
as the tree is. We will talk about this in the next lesson.
