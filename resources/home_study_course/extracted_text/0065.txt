the utter absence of evil, sin or shame. There is no thought of such an unbalanced
condition while in the one ecstatic condition of perfect balance which the Light of love is.
     When I say there can be no thought of such a thing I can best transfer that thought to
you by asking if you feel the slightest thought of falling while you stand in balance? You
obviously cannot, for your senses will not vibrate to tell you you are falling until you
actually begin to fall.
     Likewise, when you are perfectly comfortable your senses will not tell you that you
are uncomfortable until you begin to be.
     You will then put on a coat if you are cold to bring your body back into the balanced
condition of comfort.
