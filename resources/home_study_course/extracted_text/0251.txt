     There is really very little to know but it has such vast extensions that it is difficult to
keep from being confused. Gradually, however, the principle of The Divine Trinity will
become so much a part of you that one cloud of confusion after another will pass away
until you can view the whole Cosmos as ONE WHOLE. When that day comes "all things
shall be added unto you" and you shall know the glory of it.
