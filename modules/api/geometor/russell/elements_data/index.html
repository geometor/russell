geometor.russell.elements_data¶

Structured 9-Octave Periodic Element data for Walter Russell’s Cosmogony.

Maps Russell’s nine octaves of matter (from primordial Hydrogen/Inert-Gas seeds through Carbon, Silicon, and the heavy radioactive octaves) with their locking tones (0, 1+, 2+, 3+, 4±, 3-, 2-, 1-), polarity classes, and gyroscopic angles.
