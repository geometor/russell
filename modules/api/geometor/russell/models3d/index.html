geometor.russell.models3d¶

Parametric 3D geometry generators for Walter Russell’s Wave-Field Cosmogony.

Almost all 180 chalkboard figures in Russell’s Home Study Course (1950–1952) are orthographic slices or camera projections of five coupled 3D structures:

  1. CubeSphereLattice (Figs. 105–124): Bounding Cube [-1, 1]^3 of Still Magnetic Light, inscribed unit Sphere, 27 zero-nodes {-1, 0, 1}^3, 3 orthogonal zero-curvature planes, and 6 diagonal bisecting zero-curvature planes (total = 9 Equators).

  2. ConicalVortexField (Figs. 38–47, 117–124, 158–161): Six centripetal (+) cones projecting inward from the 6 cube faces to the common gravitational apex (0, 0, 0), paired with centrifugal (-) unwinding spirals and curved pressure equators.

  3. LightSphereLens (Figs. 170–172): 3D octant-divided optical wave-field whose field trajectories pass through zero curvature (kappa = 0) on the 3 orthogonal equators and reverse concavity across adjacent octants while ejecting equatorial rings.

  4. GyroscopicOctaveSystem (Figs. 95–104): Nine gyroscopic precession stages (0 -> 1+ -> 2+ -> 3+ -> 4± -> 3- -> 2- -> 1- -> 0) precessing from 0° to 90° (True Sphere at Wave Amplitude 4±) to 180° while morphing from prolate spheroids to a true sphere to oblate ringed systems.

  5. PeriodicHelix9Octave (Figs. 177, 179–180): Unified 3D 9-octave conical helical wave of the elements whose axial camera projection reproduces Russell’s concentric chart (Fig. 177) and whose lateral camera projection reproduces the corkscrew octave wave (Fig. 180).

Build the Unified 3D Nine-Octave Conical Spiral of the Elements (Figs. 177 & 180).

Coordinate design unifies Russell’s two periodic charts into ONE 3D geometry: - Axial Projection ``(X, Y)`` looking down the Z wave axis reproduces

The Russell Periodic Chart of the Elements, No. 2 (Fig. 177, 1926): concentric expanding spiral turns with Inert Gases on the horizontal Y = 0 Zero-Equator and Carbon/Silicon/etc. on the vertical X = 0, Y > 0 4± Crest.

  • Lateral Projection ``(Z, Y)`` looking perpendicular to the Z wave axis reproduces the 9-Octave Wave Arch & Corkscrew Vortex (Figs. 179–180)!
