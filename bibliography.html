Chronological Register of Primary Works by Walter & Lao Russell and Related Treatises

Primary Works by Walter & Lao Russell¶

  • Russell, Walter (1926). The Universal One: An Exact Science of the One Visible and Invisible Universe of Mind and the Registration of All Idea of Thinking Mind in Light, Which Is Matter and Also Energy. New York: Brieger Press.

  • Russell, Walter (1926). The Russell Periodic Chart of the Elements, No. 1 & No. 2 (reproduced in Fig. 177 of Lesson 47).

  • Russell, Walter (1930). The Russell Genero-Radiative Concept, or The Cyclic Theory of Continuous Motion. New York: L. Middleditch Co.

  • Russell, Walter (1947). The Secret of Light. Waynesboro, VA: University of Science and Philosophy.

  • Russell, Walter (1948–1949). The Message of the Divine Iliad, Vols. I–II. Waynesboro, VA: University of Science and Philosophy.

  • Russell, Walter & Lao Russell (1950–1952). Home Study Course in Universal Law, Natural Science and Philosophy (Units 1–12, Lessons 1–48, 3rd Edition). Waynesboro, VA: Walter Russell Foundation.

  • Russell, Walter (1953). A New Concept of the Universe. Waynesboro, VA: University of Science and Philosophy.

  • Russell, Walter & Lao Russell (1957). Atomic Suicide? Waynesboro, VA: University of Science and Philosophy.

  • Mendeleev, Dmitri Ivanovich (1904). An Attempt Towards a Chemical Conception of the Ether. Trans. George Kamensky. London: Longmans, Green, and Co.

  • Duncan, Robert Kennedy (1905). The New Knowledge: A Popular Endeavor to Set Forth the New Physics and the New Chemistry. New York: A. S. Barnes & Co.
