Walter Russell (1871–1963)
==========================

American polymath, sculptor, painter, architect, musician, and natural philosopher; author of *The Universal One* (1926), *The Secret of Light* (1947), and co-author with Lao Russell of the *Home Study Course* (1950–1952).
