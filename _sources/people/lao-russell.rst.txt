Lao Russell
===========

**Lao Russell** (*1904–1988*)
   Philosopher, author, and co-founder with Walter Russell of the University of Science and Philosophy at Swannanoa Palace, Waynesboro, Virginia. Co-author of the 48-lesson *Home Study Course* (1950–1952) and *Atomic Suicide?* (1957).
