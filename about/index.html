About¶

Extracting Historical Semantic Knowledge & Repurposing It for Exact Symbolic Reasoning

About This Edition¶

The GEOMETOR edition of Walter Russell and Lao Russell establishes a comprehensive digital critical edition and parametric visual laboratory uniting the 12 Units and 48 Lessons of their Home Study Course in Universal Law, Natural Science and Philosophy (1950–1952) with a newly synthesized geometric treatise, The Wave-Field Cosmogony. Walter Russell’s cosmogony articulates a continuous, two-way, genero-radiative universe governed by rhythmic balanced interchange: unfolding from the Still Magnetic Light of Mind (the motionless fulcrum 0) through opposing pairs of six-axis conical vortices (centripetal generative compression + and centrifugal radiative expansion -), into cubic wave-fields bounded by nine zero-curvature equators, eight-octant spherical light-lenses, gyroscopic precessional amplitude planes, and a unified nine-octave periodic table of the elements.

This digital edition extracts the implicit deductive and visual architecture of the Russells’ corpus into an addressable, interactive knowledge graph. All 48 lessons, student dialogues, and 102 original diagram plates (Figs. 1–180) are indexed with stable anchors, paired with high-resolution parametric vector reconstructions, and equipped with interactive 3D WebGL models and downloadable Wavefront .obj meshes. By elevating hand-drawn chalkboard diagrams into mathematically calibrated vector geometry, the edition allows students and researchers to inspect wave mechanics from any vantage point.

Within the broader GEOMETOR research matrix, this edition connects the Russells’ wave-field geometry to classical and modern mathematical corpora: the Platonic solids and Euclidean proportion in geometor.elements, golden ratio spirals and octave scaling in geometor.divine, biological morphogenesis and coordinate transformations in geometor.thompson, rational trigonometry invariants in geometor.wildberger, and exact symbolic modeling in geometor.model.

The GEOMETOR Mission¶

GEOMETOR is an ongoing research initiative founded by phi ARCHITECT dedicated to recovering, formalizing, and revitalizing the world’s mathematical and geometric heritage through three foundational pillars:

  1. Extracting Historical Semantic Knowledge: Moving beyond passive page scans and OCR to recover the living deductive structure of 2,300+ years of geometric, axiomatic, and philosophical treatises—elevating prose, marginal citations, primary-source folios (via publish folio), and calibrated Ink-to-Vector .euclid-graphic diagrams into structured, machine-addressable semantic graphs.

  2. Exact Symbolic Grounding (``geometor.model``): Anchoring geometric constructions in 100% exact symbolic algebra (SymPy) and Rational Trigonometry invariants (quadrance, spread, cross-ratio) over exact algebraic fields rather than floating-point approximations. In Russell’s cosmogony, this symbolic grounding enables exact parametric representation of conical angles, octahedral planes, and harmonic octave divisions.

  3. Repurposing Knowledge Across Mediums: Re-deploying extracted semantic and algebraic structures across synchronized multi-track Book Codices and EPUB 3 editions (photon-platform/publish), computable deductive canons (geometor.elements, geometor.etc, geometor.pappus, geometor.divine), interactive SVG laboratories (geometor.explorer), QHD visual broadcasts, and neuro-symbolic program synthesis (geometor.seer) across geometor.com.

Sources, Provenance & Methodology¶

The textual materials, diagram plates, and periodic charts in this edition derive from original editions published by the Walter Russell Foundation and the University of Science and Philosophy (Waynesboro, Virginia):

  • Russell, Walter (1926): The Universal One. New York: Brieger Press.

  • Russell, Walter (1926): The Russell Periodic Chart of the Elements, No. 1 & No. 2.

  • Russell, Walter (1930): The Russell Genero-Radiative Concept. New York: L. Middleditch Co.

  • Russell, Walter (1947): The Secret of Light. Waynesboro, VA: University of Science and Philosophy.

  • Russell, Walter (1948–1949): The Message of the Divine Iliad, Vols. I–II.

  • Russell, Walter & Lao Russell (1950–1952): Home Study Course in Universal Law, Natural Science and Philosophy (Units 1–12, Lessons 1–48, 3rd Edition).

  • Russell, Walter (1953): A New Concept of the Universe.

Every text in this edition follows the Digital Scriptorium methodology: preserving authorial terminology and diagram numbering while providing modern interactive accessibility, side-by-side vector diagrams, and 3D geometric visualizations.
