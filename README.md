## Overview

`geometor.russell` is a digital reconstruction and geometric exploration of the cosmogony of **Walter and Lao Russell**, centered on the 12-unit, 48-lesson *Home Study Course in Universal Law, Natural Science and Philosophy* (1950–1952) and its 180 diagrams (`Fig. 1`–`Fig. 180`).

### Core Focus Areas

1. **Multi-Track Book Codex (`docsrc/`)**:
   - Complete digital edition of the 48 Lessons across 12 Units, paired with companion cosmogony synthesis, semantic glossary (`:term:`), prosopography (`:person:`), and cross-work bibliography (`:cite:`).
2. **Parametric Vector Reconstruction of the 180 Figures**:
   - Recovery of archival plates (`resources/home_study_course/`), de-compositing of multi-figure chalkboard plates, and programmatic SVG reconstruction with adaptive light/dark-mode styling.
3. **3D Wave-Field Geometry & Animations**:
   - **The Cube-Sphere Wave-Field (`Figs. 105–124`)**: The 9 planes of zero curvature (3 orthogonal + 6 diagonal equators) bounding curved electric motion.
   - **Opposing Spiral Cones (`Figs. 38–47, 62–71, 158–161`)**: Centripetal generation (winding into incandescent spheres at wave amplitude) vs. centrifugal radiation (unwinding back into cubic zero-planes).
   - **Spherical Light-Lenses (`Figs. 170–172`)**: Three orthogonal equators of curvature reversal across the eight octants.
   - **The 9-Octave Periodic Wave of Matter (`Figs. 103, 175–180`)**: Helical octave wave from Inert Gas master-tones (`0`) through `1+, 2+, 3+` to Carbon amplitude (`4+-4`) and `3-, 2-, 1-`.

## Repository Structure

- `resources/home_study_course/`:
  - `epub/`: Unpacked clean XHTML (`OEBPS/Text/lesson_01.xhtml`–`lesson_48.xhtml`, `prelude.xhtml`) and 102 embedded plates.
  - `pdfs/`: Original 12 Unit PDFs (`part_01.pdf`–`part_12.pdf`).
  - `docx_media/`: 179 un-downsampled source JPEGs/PNGs extracted from `part_01`–`part_12`.
  - `Russell-HomeStudyCourse.epub` & `Russell-HomeStudyCourse.pdf`: Complete compiled editions.
- `docsrc/`: Sphinx Book Codex source files for `photon-platform/publish`.
- `src/geometor/russell/`: Python package for text compilation, SVG diagram generation, and 3D cosmogony models.
